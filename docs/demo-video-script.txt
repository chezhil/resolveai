RESOLVEAI - 3 MINUTE DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
======================================

BEFORE YOU RECORD
-----------------
- Open: resolveai-production-99b1.up.railway.app/?room=video
- Browser window 1280x800 or bigger, zoom 100%
- In the console, click Reset once
- Model outage toggle: OFF
- Skip the audit tamper button (it's switched off on the server)
- Speak a bit slower than feels natural


0:00 - 0:20  HOOK AND PROBLEM
-----------------------------
SCREEN: Landing page hero. Hold still, then slowly
scroll to "A stuck payment hides three problems".

SAY:
"A customer pays a small Paytm merchant. The
soundbox stays silent. Is the money lost, or just
late? The merchant doesn't know. Support guesses,
promises a time it can't keep, and sometimes
refunds on a bank status that changed minutes
later."


0:20 - 0:40  WHAT RESOLVEAI IS
------------------------------
SCREEN: Scroll to "Five steps. Every case." Let the
Hear > Plan > Check > Act > Verify animation play.

SAY:
"ResolveAI is an AI support teammate for Paytm
merchants. Every case runs one loop: hear, plan,
check, act, verify. The AI only suggests what to
do. A policy engine written in code decides. And a
case closes only when the ledger proves the money
moved."


0:40 - 1:25  SCENARIO A - THE SILENT SOUNDBOX
---------------------------------------------
SCREEN: Click "Open live demo". In the console,
click scenario A "Manjunath's Rs 480". Let it run.

SAY:
"Here's Manjunath. He sends a voice note in
Kannada."

>> Point at the HEAR step (message with personal
   data hidden)

"Before any AI sees it, phone numbers and UPI IDs
are encrypted. This is all the model ever sees."

>> Point at the green ALLOW rows, the rule IDs and
   the lightning-bolt microseconds

"It checks the ledger, the Paytm gateway and the
bank, finds the stuck 480 rupees, and raises a bank
ticket. That's level 1: small, so it acts on its
own. Every action passes fifteen code rules, and
that check takes microseconds."

>> Point at the case panel: exact time, watcher,
   RBI clock

"It tells Manjunath, in Kannada, exactly when the
money lands, and sets a watcher on itself."

>> Click "+1 day"

"Next morning, the bank credits. The agent re-reads
the ledger, and only then closes the case.
Verified, not assumed."


1:25 - 1:55  SCENARIO B - TWO-PERSON RULE
-----------------------------------------
SCREEN: Click B "Priya's Rs 1.84 L settlement".
Wait for the approval card.

SAY:
"Bigger money needs a human. Priya's 1.84 lakh
settlement is held over a name mismatch. The agent
runs a bank-account check, then stops at level 2."

>> Click "Approve as Ops lead"
>> Then click "Approve as Risk lead"

"Above one lakh, two different people must sign.
One person can't sign twice, and the AI can never
approve itself."


1:55 - 2:15  SCENARIO D - ATTACK BLOCKED
----------------------------------------
SCREEN: Click D "Change my payout account, urgent".
Let it escalate.

SAY:
"Now an attacker: 'urgent, change my payout
account.' Rule P1 blocks it outright. Payout
accounts can never be changed from chat. The fraud
desk gets a full case file, so nobody has to ask
the merchant again."


2:15 - 2:35  SCENARIO H - LATE BANK MESSAGE
-------------------------------------------
SCREEN: Click H "Rs 4,500 paid twice, bank reverses
one late". When the approval card shows, click
"simulated late reversal". Show the quarantine
card, then flip the middle panel to "Provenance"
for 3 seconds.

SAY:
"This is the part we're proudest of. A refund waits
for approval. Then the bank reverses one payment
late. Every approval remembers the facts it rests
on, so it's frozen instantly. No human can sign off
on a picture that's no longer true."


2:35 - 2:48  MODEL OUTAGE
-------------------------
SCREEN: Turn ON the "Model outage" toggle. Run A
again. Show the yellow "Degraded mode" banner.

SAY:
"And if Groq and Gemini both go down? A
deterministic planner takes over through the same
rules. Merchants don't notice. If a model dies
after moving money, we never replay it; a human
takes over."


2:48 - 3:00  PROOF AND THE ASK
------------------------------
SCREEN: Open the Analytics (or Evals) tab: 35/35
and 100% vs 79%. End on the landing hero or logo.

SAY:
"35 out of 35 test cases, all 11 attacks blocked,
and the right call on every replayed ticket,
against 79% for humans. The tickets are synthetic
and the Paytm systems are simulated. Our ask:
sandbox APIs and a 30-day shadow pilot. ResolveAI:
every stuck rupee, resolved and verified."


RECORDING TIPS
--------------
- Running long? Cut H's Provenance view first, then
  the model outage part. Never cut scenario A.
- Scenarios take 8-12 seconds. Talk over them, or
  speed those bits up 1.5x when editing.
- Easiest: record the voiceover first, then fit the
  screen clips to it.
- Between takes click Reset, or use a new room
  (?room=video2).
- Add a small corner caption the whole time:
  "Simulated Paytm environment - synthetic data"
